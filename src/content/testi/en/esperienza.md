*Omaggio a Ennio Morricone* is designed for a broad audience. The melodies belong to the history of cinema, but above all to the memory of different generations: those who saw the films at the cinema, those who discovered the music later, and those meeting this repertoire for the first time.

Because the themes are so recognisable, the show is immediately accessible, while its symphonic dimension lets the audience rediscover them in all their depth. The result is an event that is at once popular and refined, spectacular and intimate, cinematic and deeply musical.

It is a tribute that does not merely seek to remember Ennio Morricone, but to keep his artistic world alive. On stage the music becomes living matter once more: it breathes through the orchestra, meets the voice, converses with the images and gives back to the audience that extraordinary power to move that has made Morricone’s compositions part of everyone’s cultural heritage.
