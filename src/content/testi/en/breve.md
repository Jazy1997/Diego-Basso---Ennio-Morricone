*Omaggio a Ennio Morricone* is a tribute conceived in 2004 by conductor Diego Basso. The Orchestra Ritmico Sinfonica Italiana, soprano Claudia Sasso and images on the big screen take the audience through Ennio Morricone’s music for cinema: from *Cinema Paradiso* to *The Mission* and the westerns of Sergio Leone.
