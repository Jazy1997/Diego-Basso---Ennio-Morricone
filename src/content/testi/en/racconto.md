titolo: "A concert that becomes a story"
ordine: 4
lang: en
---

The strength of *Omaggio a Ennio Morricone* lies in its ability to turn the concert into a true story. Each piece opens a scene, recalls a character, rebuilds an atmosphere. The music does not simply accompany the images: it completes them and, at the same time, lives on its own on stage.

The alternation of great symphonic pages, more intimate moments and vocal passages creates a continuous emotional arc. The audience moves from the epic scale of Sergio Leone’s westerns to the tenderness of *Cinema Paradiso*, from the spirituality of *The Mission* to the suspended, melancholy atmospheres of *Once Upon a Time in America*.

In the version with film projections, this journey gains even greater theatrical force: the big screen and the orchestra share the same space, taking the audience back inside the cinema through the unrepeatable experience of music performed live.
