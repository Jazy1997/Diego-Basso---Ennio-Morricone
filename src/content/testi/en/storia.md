Conceived in 2004, the project has over the years been taken to theatres, squares and places of particular scenic and cultural value, always keeping one defining feature: to bring the uniqueness of Morricone’s music together with the uniqueness of the space that hosts it.

The repertoire has been performed in very different settings: from the Teatro Malibran in Venice to the Teatro Mario Del Monaco in Treviso, from the Teatro Verdi in Padua to open-air venues such as the Bridge of Bassano del Grappa, the beach by the lighthouse in Bibione, the historic walls and squares of the Veneto, villas and natural landscapes. The project has also had an international dimension: in the 2015/2016 season Diego Basso was chosen by the city of Guangzhou, China, to conduct the Guangzhou Symphony Orchestra for its Christmas Concert.

This long experience makes *Omaggio a Ennio Morricone* a project that adapts to different settings: theatres, festivals, cultural seasons, major events and monumental locations, always keeping the quality of the orchestral performance and the direct connection with the audience at its centre.
