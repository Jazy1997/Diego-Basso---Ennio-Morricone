The show is born from the meeting between the full orchestra and Ennio Morricone’s cinematic imagination. His melodies, by now part of our collective memory, return to the stage in a symphonic journey across eras, films, characters and moods. This is not simply a matter of performing famous soundtracks: the aim is to recreate, through sound, Morricone’s unique ability to turn a scene into an emotion you can recognise even with your eyes closed. With transcriptions by Maestro Diego Basso, who also conducts, the Orchestra Ritmico Sinfonica Italiana and soprano Claudia Sasso, orchestra, voice and cinema become a single experience.
