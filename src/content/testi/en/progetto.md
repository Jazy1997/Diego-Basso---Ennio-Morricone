*Omaggio a Ennio Morricone* is a musical project conceived by Maestro Diego Basso in 2004 and dedicated to the extraordinary artistic legacy of one of the composers who have most profoundly shaped the history of cinema and of the music of our time.

The show is born from the meeting between the full orchestra and Ennio Morricone’s cinematic imagination. His melodies, by now part of our collective memory, are brought back to the stage in a symphonic journey that moves across eras, films, characters and moods. This is not simply a matter of performing famous soundtracks: the aim is to recreate, through sound, Morricone’s unique ability to turn a film scene into an emotion you can recognise even with your eyes closed.

The project is built around transcriptions by Maestro Diego Basso and on the experience he has gained over more than twenty years of concerts devoted to Morricone’s world. The music is entrusted to the Orchestra Ritmico Sinfonica Italiana, 46 musicians strong, with lyric soprano Claudia Sasso and conducted by Maestro Basso himself.

In the version with projections, images from the films enter into dialogue with the live performance and heighten the show’s narrative character: orchestra, voice and cinema become parts of a single experience, able to move the audience musically as much as visually and emotionally.
