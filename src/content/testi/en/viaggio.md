The programme takes in some of the best-known pages of Ennio Morricone’s output, chosen from a catalogue of more than four hundred film scores. The concert leads the audience through cinematic worlds that are very different from one another, bringing out the extraordinary range of the Roman composer’s writing.

From *Cinema Paradiso* to *Malèna*, from *The Mission* to *The Legend of 1900*, from *Sacco & Vanzetti* to *Once Upon a Time in America*, all the way to the unforgettable collaborations with Sergio Leone: *Once Upon a Time in the West*, *The Good, the Bad and the Ugly*, *A Fistful of Dynamite*, *A Fistful of Dollars* and *For a Few Dollars More*.

Themes such as “Gabriel’s Oboe”, “The Ecstasy of Gold”, “Chi Mai”, “Playing Love” and the melodies of *Cinema Paradiso* are not just soundtracks: they have become a universal language, able to summon images, memories and feelings at once. It is precisely this bond between musical memory and cinematic memory that lies at the heart of the show.
