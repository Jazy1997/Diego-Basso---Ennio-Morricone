With a deep knowledge of the orchestra and its range of colours, Diego Basso approaches Morricone’s repertoire by placing the richness of the original writing and its extraordinary narrative power at the centre. His work as transcriber and conductor is designed to convey live the complexity, dynamics and nuances of the compositions, bringing out the interplay between strings, woodwinds, brass, percussion, rhythm section and voice.

Maestro Basso’s relationship with this repertoire goes back a long way. His collaborations over the years include work with Alessandro Alessandroni, the celebrated performer of the famous whistle in *A Fistful of Dollars*, and with the internationally renowned flautist Andrea Griminelli.

In 2021 Diego Basso also arranged and conducted “Lost Boys Calling”, a composition by Ennio Morricone and Roger Waters featured on Zucchero’s album *Discover*. The same year saw the release of the albums *Omaggio a Ennio Morricone* and *Griminelli plays Morricone*, testimony to an artistic path firmly rooted in this repertoire.
