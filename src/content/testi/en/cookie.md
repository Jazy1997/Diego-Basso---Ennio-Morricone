The site does not set profiling, advertising or analytics cookies, and does not use tools that track visitors. That is why there is no consent banner.

## Technical cookies

The site does not need cookies to work while you browse. Should strictly technical cookies ever be needed (for example for the security of the contact form), they would not require consent and would be listed here.

## Embedded content

The YouTube video and the Spotify player **do not load until you click** to start them: before that, your browser does not contact their servers. Once started, YouTube (in privacy-enhanced mode, youtube-nocookie.com) and Spotify may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

## Managing cookies

You can view and delete cookies in your browser settings. To find out how personal data is processed, read the privacy notice.
