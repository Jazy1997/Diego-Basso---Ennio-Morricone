This notice explains how we process the personal data of visitors to the site and of people who write to us using the contact form, under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

## What data we process

- **Data sent with the contact form**: full name, email address, subject of the request and the text of the message.
- **Technical browsing data**: IP address, date and time of the request, page visited and browser type. These are recorded by the hosting provider in its standard server logs, for the operation and security of the site.

The site does not use profiling cookies or analytics tools that identify visitors. Details are on the Cookies page.

## Why and on what legal basis

- **Replying to requests** sent with the form: based on the consent you give by ticking the box and, for booking requests, on pre-contractual steps taken at your request (Art. 6(1)(a) and (b) GDPR).
- **Security and operation of the site**: our legitimate interest in protecting the site from abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). For this reason the form limits the number of submissions from the same IP address.

## Who receives the data

The data is processed by providers acting as data processors:

- **Vercel Inc.** (United States): hosting of the site and of the function that receives the form.
- **Resend** (United States): delivery of the form message to our mailbox.

Transfers to the United States are based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission. The data is never sold or used for advertising.

## Third-party content

The YouTube video (in privacy-enhanced mode, youtube-nocookie.com) and the Spotify player load **only when you click** to start them. From that moment the privacy notices of Google and Spotify apply. The links to Apple Music, Tidal and social networks are simple external links.

## How long we keep it

Messages received through the form are kept for as long as needed to reply and follow up on the request, and in any case for no longer than 24 months. Technical logs are kept by the hosting provider for short periods, according to its policies.

## Your rights

You may at any time request access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and portability, and object to processing; you may withdraw your consent without affecting processing already carried out. To do so, write to the data controller at the address shown above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (garanteprivacy.it).
